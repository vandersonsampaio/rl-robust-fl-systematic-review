# Reliability

`reconciliation_rules.md` describes the reliability procedure, how disagreements were resolved and the coding rules, and lists the files of this folder. `interrater_agreement.csv` summarizes agreement per round and stage. `scripts/validate_counts.py` recomputes every row of it from the decision-level files.

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `interrater_agreement.csv` | agreement, kappa and PABAK per round and stage |
| `stage1_sample_key.csv`, `stage1_blind_sample.csv` | stage-1 calibration and blind sample, with R1 and R2 decisions |
| `r2_round1_*.csv` | R2's round-1 coding: stage-2 exclusions and inclusions, RQ2 and quality |
| `stage2_consensus.csv` | stage-2 disagreements of round 1 and the final decisions |
| `r2_round2_sample_key.csv` | studies in the round-2 samples and their stratum |
| `r2_round2_rq2_agreement.csv` | RQ2 coding of R1 (before and after consensus) and R2, side by side; `r2_no_defense = S` marks the seven studies R2 coded as "fixed action (no defense)", which counts as a fixed action in the agreement and as no comparison after amendment A20 |
| `r2_round2_quality_agreement.csv` | quality scores of R1 (before consensus) and R2 per study and criterion, with R2's round-1 score where it exists |
| `sensitivity_analysis.md` | sensitivity analysis of the RQ2 and quality counts, generated by `scripts/sensitivity.py` |
| `reconciliation_rules.md` | procedure, reconciliation and coding rules |
| `codebook/` | written codebook: R1's copy, R2's copy as sent, and R1's boundary cases, in Portuguese |
| `original_pt/` | agreement analyses, consensus decisions, comparison of R1's reading notes with the preliminary extraction, and the original sensitivity analysis, in Portuguese |

## Sensitivity analysis

`original_pt/sensibilidade_qp2_qa.md` was generated on October 19–21, 2026, before amendment A27 (October 22), and its baseline row shows 14 closest comparisons with 6 ties. `sensitivity_analysis.md` repeats the same scenarios on the final coding of this repository, with A27 applied: the baseline row matches the paper (15 closest comparisons, 7 ties, 23 studies with a fixed action, heuristic or oracle), and conclusions A–E hold in every scenario, as in the original.
